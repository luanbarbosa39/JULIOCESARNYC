WATERS OF MARCH: THE COLLECTION

Inspired by the timeless bossa nova masterpiece by António Carlos Jobim, Waters of March is a collection that carries moments of surreal poetry — woven into the garments and captured in every photograph. Literally and figuratively, we were in the Waters of March.

To bring our vision to life, we traveled to Belém, the historic capital of Pará in northern Brazil. Founded in 1616 and standing proudly at the mouth of the Amazon River, Belém is a city where colonial architecture meets the vibrant, untamed pulse of the rainforest. It is a place of profound cultural richness, blending Indigenous and European influences into a unique atmosphere.

We were, quite literally, in the waters of March. It rained every day as we scouted the city and its surroundings for locations, and in the end, we shot most of this collection under pouring skies. But the storms were figurative, too. On our very first day in Belém, I suffered a detached retina and lost vision in my right eye, requiring emergency surgery the following morning. By the third day, a severe allergic reaction to local cuisine left me hospitalized, voiceless, and relying on adrenaline injections to recover. Amidst this, members of our crew were quietly navigating their own personal turmoils. Yet, it is in the darkest storms that true colors shine. I have always believed that "friends will be friends," a phrase that represents not just shared joy, but shared burdens. When I was physically unable to be on set, my incredible team stepped up. Under the guidance of our Production Coordinator, Bert de Liefde, they took over the project and executed it flawlessly.

This collection is dedicated to them, to Ravanelli Mesquita, Thiago Lobato, Juan Ripoll, Bert de Liefde, and Marcelo Migs, also to my atelier team — Eudocia Medeiros, Renata Silva, Anne and Tauan Linhares. I have no words to fully express my respect and appreciation. Thank you for being there for me. I love you as friends, and I am in awe of the work you do. Waters of March is as much yours as it is mine.

Waters of March is more than a fashion collection; it is a celebration of artistic perseverance and the profound power of friendship.
